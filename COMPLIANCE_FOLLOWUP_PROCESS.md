# Compliance Follow-up & Escalation Process

## Objective
Ensure recurring IT compliance activities are completed accurately, on time, and with sufficient evidence.

## Workflow
1. Maintain a compliance calendar with owner, frequency, due date and required evidence.
2. Review upcoming deadlines weekly.
3. Send reminders before due dates where activities remain incomplete.
4. Validate submitted evidence for completeness and relevance.
5. Record completion date and evidence location.
6. Log missed deadlines or control failures as exceptions.
7. Assign an exception owner and target remediation date.
8. Escalate overdue or high-risk items to the appropriate lead.
9. Track remediation to closure and retain evidence.
10. Review recurring exceptions for process-improvement opportunities.

## Process improvement ideas
- Standardize evidence checklists
- Automate overdue reminders
- Add privileged-access-specific review criteria
- Track recurring failure categories
- Define clear escalation thresholds
- Maintain procedure revision history
