# User Access Certification Runbook

## Purpose
Provide a repeatable process for reviewing and certifying user access.

## Procedure
1. Obtain the current access extract and confirm the population is complete.
2. Reconcile users against employment status and application ownership.
3. Identify privileged access, stale access, terminated users, and overdue reviews.
4. Route access items to the appropriate manager or application owner.
5. Record a decision: Approve, Revoke, Modify, or Pending.
6. Follow up on incomplete decisions before the due date.
7. Escalate overdue or high-risk items according to the defined escalation path.
8. Verify completed removals/changes and retain supporting evidence.
9. Update the exception log and close items only after remediation is validated.
10. Summarize completion metrics, high-risk findings, and recurring issues.

## Evidence examples
- Access extract
- Reviewer certification
- Manager/application-owner approval
- Ticket or change record
- Access-removal confirmation
- Exception closure evidence

## Quality checks
- Every access item has an owner and reviewer
- Every decision is dated and traceable
- Terminated users are investigated immediately
- Privileged access receives heightened review
- Exceptions include owner, target date and resolution evidence
